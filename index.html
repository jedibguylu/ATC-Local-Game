Each shift picks the runway configuration and traffic pattern at random. Weather changes as you work: VFR, haze, rain under a low ceiling, or fog. When the field goes IFR, the pattern closes. Time of day runs on a 10-minute day, so you'll work sunset, night, and sunrise every shift. In low visibility you see only lights past the tower's visibility, plus whatever the DBRITE and data tags show.

HOW TO WORK THE POSITION

Basics

ClickSelect an aircraft: a target, its data tag, a strip, a DBRITE target, or a line in the frequency log.
TabJump to the next pilot waiting on you. Amber tags and strips are calling, and the strip shows how long they've waited.
1 – 0Press the matching button in the aircraft panel.
SpacePause. 1× / 2× / 4× in the top bar.
EscDeselect.

Building a taxi route

DestinationPick a runway entry (full length or an intersection), the assigned gate/spot, or click a gate or spot on the map.
RouteClick taxiways on the map in the order you want them. The amber dashed line previews the route. Leave it empty for the auto route (in via D, out via E).
EnterSend the taxi clearance. Backspace removes the last taxiway.
Hold shortEvery runway crossing stops at the hold line until you press Cross. Use "Hold short of…" for a taxiway on the route.
GridlockAircraft stop for traffic and call "we're stopped". Give one a new route the other way and it will turn around.

Tower

DeparturesLine up and wait, cleared for takeoff (IFR heading defaults to the strip; VFR pilots choose pattern work or on-course themselves), cancel takeoff clearance (rejects if rolling), contact departure.
IFR arrivalsCleared to land, go around, final approach speed, 160–180 kt to a fix, expedite exit, plan exit.
VFRPilots ask for touch & go or full stop; clear them for it. Enter left/right downwind or base, straight-in, make left/right traffic (applies from upwind or the next circuit), extend downwind, turn base, short approach, 360s, caution wake. Follow: the pilot extends until the traffic turns base, then turns base where it did and follows it down final.
RolloutExpedite runway exit (the pilot brakes harder and takes the first exit they can), or turn at a specific exit.
